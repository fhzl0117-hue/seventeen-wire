SEOUL — AUGUST 2026

Thirteen voices,
one long pause.

SEVENTEEN is deep into a mass military hiatus. Jeonghan, Wonwoo, Hoshi, and Woozi are already serving; Vernon began his alternative service on August 20, marking it with a self-written single, "I'll Be Back"; DK enlists September 8; Mingyu begins alternative service September 10; and Seungkwan and Dino follow together on October 26. Jeonghan, the first member discharged, is already lining up an October unit debut with Joshua. Dino's solo alter-ego Picheolin closed out August with a new single, "LIKE IT," and a first overseas solo concert confirmed for Macao on October 10 — likely his last show before enlistment. At their June "Carat Land" fan meeting, Mingyu told fans the members expect to reunite in two to three years — the start of what he called a "second chapter." This page is a working build of how SEVENTEEN Wire tracks that timeline for English-speaking CARAT — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

3.8 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"A hyperpop detour that trades stakes for texture — pleasant company for twenty-two minutes, memorable for maybe three of them."

V8 (2026): eight tracks, the debut EP from Vernon and The8's new duo project. Released June 29 in the middle of SEVENTEEN's mass enlistment wave, the title track "singasong" was co-written with German producer Mechatok and rides a hyperpop synth line built around one insistent hook. Elsewhere on the tracklist, Pharrell Williams produced "girlsnboys," and the two members split songwriting across English, Mandarin, and Korean — each choosing whichever language felt most natural to write in.

Reception has landed in the middle: K-pop review outlet The Bias List scored the title track 7.75 out of 10, praising the hook while arguing it plays more like a sketch than a finished idea. It's a curiosity project more than a statement — which suits a duo filling time between enlistments rather than trying to define an era.
